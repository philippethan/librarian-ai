Science Lights the Way 


In 1998 British researcher Andrew Wakefield and his colleagues published
a paper showing a link between the measles-mumps-rubella vaccine and the incidence
of autism in children. During the following years the paper was exposed as an
elaborate fraud and was retracted by the Lancet, the journal that published it. Dozens
of follow-up studies have since shown zero connection between vaccines and autism.
