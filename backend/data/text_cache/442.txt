Introduction 
 
 
This book contains all the most 
important words that you need 
to know to successfully complete 
speaking and writing/essay 
parts of IELTS, TOEIC, and 
TOEFL tests. 
 
This material is perfect for any 
serious candidate who does not 
wish to waste time researching 
and learning new vocabulary the 
traditional way. This book will 
make your learning more 
efficient with less of your own 
effort, which means more spare 
time to review other concepts.
