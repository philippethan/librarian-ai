Abstract
The development of railway cyber physical systems
is a challenging process. In this paper we present
our current effort to extend AADL to include new
features for separation of concerns of railway cyber
physical systems, we extend AADL in spatial aspect,
dynamic continuous aspect, physical world
modeling aspect. Finally, we illustrate the proposed
method via an example of specification of
communication based train control system.
