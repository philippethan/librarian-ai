Relationships
Few things promise us greater happiness than our relationships – yet few
things more reliably deliver misery and frustration. Our error is to suppose
that we are born knowing how to love and that managing a relationship
might therefore be intuitive and easy. This book starts from a different
premise: that love is a skill to be learnt, rather than just an emotion to be
felt. It calmly and charmingly takes us around the key issues of
relationships, from arguments to sex, forgiveness to communication,
making sure that success in love need never again be just a matter of luck.
